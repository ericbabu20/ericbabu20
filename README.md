## A little about me

**I started out building physical spaces. Now I build digital experiences.**

I'm **Eric Babu**, a Product Owner / Product Analyst based in Leicester, UK. I work across discovery, UX, delivery and analytics to make complex journeys easier for real people — particularly in the regulated UK energy market.

At **Outfox Energy (Redfish / Zog Solutions)**, I've worked on the customer portal, mobile app and onboarding journey. I translate customer pain points, business rules and API constraints into product experiences that people can use without needing to call support.

Before product, I worked in construction, sales and interior design operations. That mix of business, technical and creative thinking still shapes how I approach problems.

## How I make things happen

> **Discover the problem** → **Define the outcome** → **Design the journey** → **Prioritise** → **Ship** → **Measure and improve**

I enjoy the space between **customer needs, business constraints and technical reality**. My work typically involves discovery and research, RICE prioritisation, PRDs and acceptance criteria, Figma prototypes, API specifications, cross-functional Agile delivery, UAT and post-release analytics.

**A product isn't done when a ticket is closed. It's done when the outcome is measurable.**
