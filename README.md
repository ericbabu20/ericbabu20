## Hello there 👋

I'm **Eric Babu**, a product professional working in the UK energy sector. I turn customer problems, operational complexity and technical constraints into clear digital products that people can actually use.

My work spans customer acquisition, account management, payments, renewals, smart-energy experiences, product analytics and API-led integrations. I work across the full product lifecycle - from discovery and UX through requirements, delivery and measurement.

## My product approach

> **Discover** the real problem → **Define** the outcome → **Design** the journey → **Deliver** iteratively → **Measure** behaviour → **Improve** what matters

I care about outcomes, not feature volume. A shipped feature that customers cannot understand or that the business cannot measure is unfinished work.
