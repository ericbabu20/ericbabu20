### 👋 About me

I'm **Eric Babu**, a **Product Owner / Product Analyst** in Leicester, UK. I work across discovery, UX, delivery and analytics to make complex journeys easy for real people — especially in the regulated **UK energy market**.

At **Outfox Energy** (Redfish / Zog Solutions) I work on the customer portal, mobile app and onboarding journey — turning customer pain points, business rules and API constraints into experiences people can use without calling support.

Before product, I worked in construction, sales and interior design. That mix of business, technical and creative thinking still shapes how I solve problems.
